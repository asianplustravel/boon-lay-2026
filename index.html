PTT Metro Forest · Visitor guide

19 November 2026 · 10:00–11:30

Metro Forest map & guidebook

Open full-size map ↗
Download map & guidebook (PDF) Download English factsheet (PDF)

PTT restored a 12-rai site in Bangkok as an urban forest and learning centre. The project uses the Miyawaki method to recreate local forest habitats with native trees.

Points of interest in the guidebook

  • Rammed earth wall and green roof. See how natural soil and rooftop planting help insulate the building.
  • Seed wall and exhibition room. Look at samples of seeds planted here and learn about Bangkok’s original forests, urban growth and PTT’s restoration work.
  • Skywalk and observation tower. Compare views of the forest at different heights and look for signs of biodiversity.
  • Nature trail. Take a quiet walk and notice the sounds and textures of the forest.
